All your playlists and Liked Songs, with no song limit.

  1. Open exportify.app and log in with Spotify.
  2. Click Export All (one .zip) or Export next to a playlist.
  3. Upload the file here, then pick the playlists.

Exportify is an open-source site approved by Spotify. It runs in your browser and saves your playlists as CSV files.

A public playlist or album. In Spotify: Share → Copy link.

Spotify's public page only lists the first {{ EMBED_TRACK_LIMIT }} songs. For longer playlists use an Exportify file.

In the Spotify app on your computer, open a playlist (or Liked Songs), click a song, press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C (Cmd on a Mac), and paste here.

Importing a playlist again (same link or same name) only adds the new songs to the playlist it created before.
