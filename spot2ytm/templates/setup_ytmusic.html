YouTube Music has no public API for playlists, so spot2ytm talks to it the way your browser does, with the session of a browser where you are logged in. It stays in your local data/ folder.

Automatic

Pick the browser where you are logged in to music.youtube.com. spot2ytm reads its YouTube cookies from this computer.

On Linux your system may ask for your keyring password, which the browser uses to protect its cookies. On Windows, Chrome and Edge lock their cookies: use Firefox, or paste the headers below. Not available when running in Docker.

Or paste the headers by hand
  1. Open music.youtube.com in Chrome, Edge or Firefox and make sure you are logged in.
  2. Open the developer tools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I) and go to the Network tab.
  3. Type browse in the filter box, then click Library (or anything) on the page so a request shows up.
  4. Find a POST request to browse?… with status 200 and click it.
  5. Copy its request headers:
    • Chrome / Edge: in Headers → Request Headers, select everything from accept: */* down to the end and copy.
    • Firefox: right-click the request → Copy Value → Copy Request Headers.
  6. Paste them below. They must contain cookie and x-goog-authuser.
Is this safe?

The cookies and headers give access to your Google account session. spot2ytm keeps them in data/ytmusic_auth.json (readable only by your user) and only sends them to music.youtube.com. Never share that file. Logging out of YouTube Music in the browser invalidates them; they otherwise last a long time.
